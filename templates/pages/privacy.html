Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 • Effective immediately

1. Introduction

VendorVerse ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal information is handled in a safe and responsible manner. This policy details how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit or make a purchase through VendorVerse.

2. Information We Collect

When you interact with the VendorVerse marketplace, we collect information you provide directly to us:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, password hash, role (Customer or Vendor), and profile preferences.
  • Order & Shipping Data: Delivery recipient name, street address, city, state, postal code, and contact phone number.
  • Vendor Details: Business name, tax information, shop policies, logo/banner imagery, and product catalog items.
  • Usage & Device Metrics: IP address, device type, browser information, pages visited, and wishlist / comparison interactions.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal data to power our marketplace operations:

  • To route order details to the respective independent vendors so they can fulfill and dispatch items to you.
  • To send order status notifications, shipping confirmations, and return updates.
  • To calculate seller analytics, vendor revenue settlements, and marketplace fee reporting.
  • To maintain platform security, detect fraudulent activities, and prevent bot abuse.

4. Information Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to advertising data brokers. Your information is only shared with:

  • Order Vendors: Sellers receive only the shipping address and customer name necessary to fulfill items you ordered from their store.
  • Payment Processors: Payment gateways securely process your credit/debit/UPI payments. VendorVerse never stores raw credit card CVV codes.
  • Legal Authorities: Only when strictly required by applicable law, court order, or governmental regulation.

5. Your Data Rights

Under applicable Indian data protection standards, you have the right to inspect, update, or request the deletion of your account information. You can edit your addresses and profile details directly within your Account settings or contact us at privacy@vendorverse.in.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

VendorVerse utilizes essential session cookies and CSRF security tokens to maintain your login status, preserve your shopping cart across browsing sessions, and protect against cross-site request forgery attacks.
