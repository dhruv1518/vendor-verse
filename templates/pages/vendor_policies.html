Compliance & Guidelines

Vendor Policies & Marketplace Standards

To preserve consumer trust and maintain a fair playing field for all sellers, every vendor on VendorVerse agrees to adhere to the standards outlined below.

1. Seller Code of Conduct 2. Listing Standards 3. Fulfillment & Dispatch 4. Returns & Cancellations 5. Payments & Commission 6. Prohibited Items

1 Seller Code of Conduct

All registered vendors must maintain the highest standards of integrity, professional conduct, and transparency. Vendors must never:

  • Misrepresent item condition, manufacturing origins, or authentic brand licensing.
  • Attempt to divert buyers away from the VendorVerse checkout platform to external channels.
  • Post fake customer reviews, incentivize artificial ratings, or harass reviewers.
  • Share or exploit customer personal contact data outside of order fulfillment necessities.

2 Product Listing Standards

Accurate catalog data ensures higher conversion and minimal return rates:

  • Photography: Must feature original or studio imagery showing the exact physical product. Misleading generic mockups are prohibited.
  • Pricing: Prices must clearly state standard retail price and any discounted sale price in Indian Rupees (₹).
  • Inventory: Real stock must be maintained in the portal. Listing products with zero available units as active is a violation of listing policies.

3 Fulfillment & Dispatch Timelines

Buyers count on timely shipping updates:

  • 48-Hour Dispatch Standard: Confirmed orders must be processed and handed over to courier logistics within 48 business hours.
  • Status Updates: Vendors must promptly update item statuses to Processing and Shipped in the vendor portal so customers receive automated tracking notifications.

4 Returns, Replacements & Cancellations

Every store must define its Return and Shipping policies in the Storefront Settings panel:

  • Defective or damaged items must be replaced or refunded within 7 days of delivery report.
  • Custom or personalized products may be listed as non-returnable provided it is prominently declared on the product page.

5 Platform Fees & Settlements

VendorVerse operates with transparent platform fees:

  • Platform commission is fixed at 5% of product order value.
  • Payout settlements for delivered orders are credited directly to the vendor's designated bank account within 3 business days following the return window closure.

6 Prohibited Items

The following categories are strictly forbidden from being listed on the platform:

✕ Counterfeit or replica goods
✕ Weapons & ammunition
✕ Hazardous chemicals
✕ Prescription pharmaceuticals
